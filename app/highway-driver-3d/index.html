About Highway Driver 3D

Get ready for high-octane, endless action in your ultimate 3D driving adventure! Choose an autorickshaw, vintage car, or ambulance and race through endless levels that become more challenging over time. Dodge traffic, squeeze through tight gaps, and test your reflexes. Crash into another vehicle, and the police will chase you! Escape the pursuit, master each vehicles handling, and see how long you can survive in this thrilling, endless driving adventure.

Why Speed Slope Fans Might Like It

Highway Driver 3D is a good pick for players who like the speed pressure in Speed Slope. Instead of staying on a narrow slope, you read traffic patterns, dodge hazards, and survive longer as the road gets more crowded.
