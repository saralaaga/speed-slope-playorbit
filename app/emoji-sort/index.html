About Emoji Sort

Emoji Sort is a pattern puzzle dressed in emoji. Each grid hides a rule — a category, a sequence or an association — and one cell is missing its emoji. Read the pattern, pick the right face, and keep the streak alive.

Categories: Sorting Games · Puzzle Games

How to Play

  1. Study the filled emoji cells in the grid.
  2. Work out the hidden pattern, sequence or category.
  3. Pick the emoji that completes the missing cell.
  4. Confirm your answer and move to the next grid.
  5. Patterns get subtler as the levels climb.

Controls

  • Mouse Click / Touch — choose the missing emoji

Tips & Tricks

  • Check rows and columns separately before assuming diagonals.
  • Look for emoji categories like food, weather or faces first.
  • Counting repeats quickly reveals sequence-based rules.
