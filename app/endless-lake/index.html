About Endless Lake

Endless Lake turns a simple crossing into a timing challenge. Keep moving across narrow paths, judge each jump before the clock catches you and beat your best time in short, repeatable runs.

Categories: Reflex Games · Runner Games · Arcade Games

How to Play

  1. Start the run and read the path ahead.
  2. Time each movement across the narrow route.
  3. Avoid falling into the endless lake.
  4. Keep enough speed to beat the clock.
  5. Restart quickly to improve your time.

Controls

  • Mouse — control the runner

Tips & Tricks

  • Commit to a jump only after reading the landing.
  • Short corrections beat panic inputs.
  • Learn the rhythm of each path.
