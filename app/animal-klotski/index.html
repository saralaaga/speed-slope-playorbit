About Animal Klotski

Animal Klotski is a charming and addictive Klotski puzzle game where every move matters! Cute animals block the path, and it's up to you to slide them strategically and help the pink rabbit escape. Each level contains different animal-shaped blocks placed across the board. Perfect Animal puzzle for kids and casual lovers. Great combination of strategy, logic, and relaxation. If you enjoy logic puzzle games, you will love discovering new strategies to solve increasingly complex puzzles.
