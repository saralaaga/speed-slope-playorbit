About Nut Sort: Color Puzzle Game

Nut Sort hands you bolts loaded with mismatched nuts and asks you to reorganize them. Thread nuts onto bolts of the same color, one at a time, until every bolt is uniform. New levels add more bolts, deeper threads and extra colors to juggle.

Categories: Sorting Games · Puzzle Games

How to Play

  1. Left-click or tap a nut to select it.
  2. Move it onto another bolt to thread it on.
  3. Nuts only stack on matching colors or onto bolts with free space.
  4. Solve the puzzle when each bolt holds one color only.
  5. Handle growing bolt counts as levels progress.

Controls

  • Left-click / Tap — select a nut and move it to a bolt

Tips & Tricks

  • Clear the top of the most crowded bolt before starting new stacks.
  • Match nuts by color early — partial stacks lock up quickly.
  • Save one bolt with spare capacity for late reshuffles.
