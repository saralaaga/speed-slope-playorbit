About Office Spider Solitaire

Your objective in Office Spider Solitaire is to clear the table by creating suited sets from king to ace. The game has either one, two or four suits depending on the difficulty setting.

Categories: Classic Games · Card Games · Solitaire Games

How to Play

  1. The game begins with four stacks of six and six stacks of five cards
  2. The top card is dealt face up
  3. You can move a card by dragging it on top of a one point higher value card
  4. For example, you may move a seven of spades on top of an eight of hearts
  5. You may move several cards with consecutive values and same suit simultaneously
  6. Any card can be places on an empty lot

Controls

  • Mouse / touch — see the in-game tutorial

Tips & Tricks

  • Slow down — one rushed move ruins a good run.
  • Use hints sparingly; they cost more than they give.
