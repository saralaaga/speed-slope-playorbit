About Endless Golf

Endless Golf is a physics golf challenge built around careful aim, power control, and changing wind. Pull back, line up the shot, and adapt as each terrain and biome changes how the ball travels.

Categories: Golf Games · Sports Games · Arcade Games

How to Play

  1. Tap or click the golf ball to begin a shot.
  2. Drag backward to set direction and power.
  3. Watch the wind indicator before releasing.
  4. Reach the hole in as few shots as possible.

Controls

  • Click / tap — select the ball
  • Drag — aim and set power
  • Release — shoot

Tips & Tricks

  • Compensate for wind before adding power.
  • Use higher arcs when the terrain blocks a flat shot.
  • Do not chase perfect power until your angle is right.
  • Different biomes change how cautious you should be.
