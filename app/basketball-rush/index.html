About Basketball Rush

This isn’t just about shooting — it's about precision, patience and strategy. As you score, the game intensifies, but with helpful power-ups at your disposal, you’ll be ready for anything. Unlock a variety of unique ball skins to customize your game and add even more excitement.

Categories: Basketball Games · Arcade Games · Sports Games · Shooting Games · Runner Games · Platformer Games

How to Play

  1. Drag and release the ball to aim and shoot it into the basket
  2. The more baskets you score, the more difficult the game becomes!
  3. Use power-ups to help you in challenging moments
  4. Earn coins with every successful shot to unlock new ball skins
  5. Keep playing to beat your high score and master the game!

Controls

  • Drag — move items

Tips & Tricks

  • Pump fake first — most defenders bite on it.
  • Release at the top of the arc for the best accuracy.
