About Gems Sort Puzzle

Gems Sort Puzzle mixes sorting with block-fitting. Drag colorful gem pieces into a target grid, rotate your thinking around awkward shapes, and fill the entire outline before time runs out.

Categories: Sorting Games · Puzzle Games

How to Play

  1. Drag gem blocks from the tray toward the target grid.
  2. Place pieces so they snap into the shape cleanly.
  3. Fill the entire grid to complete the puzzle.
  4. Beat the timer for a better score.
  5. Handle bigger grids and stranger shapes at higher levels.

Controls

  • Mouse / Tap — drag and place gem blocks

Tips & Tricks

  • Place the largest, most awkward piece first.
  • Keep corners and edges for shapes that fit them exactly.
  • Scan the tray before dropping anything — order matters.
