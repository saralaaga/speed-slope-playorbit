About Go Golf

Go Golf is a compact sports puzzle game where every stroke matters. Observe the terrain, aim precisely, and send the golf ball into the hole before you run out of turns.

Categories: Golf Games · Sports Games · Puzzle Games

How to Play

  1. Use the mouse to line up each shot.
  2. Choose enough power to reach the hole or a safe setup spot.
  3. Avoid wasting strokes on rushed angles.
  4. Clear each level within the stroke limit.

Controls

  • Mouse — aim and shoot
  • Touch — mobile aim and shoot

Tips & Tricks

  • Study the terrain before the first stroke.
  • Leave yourself a simple second shot when the direct route is risky.
  • Soft shots are easier to correct than overhit ones.
  • Use walls only when they create a cleaner final angle.
