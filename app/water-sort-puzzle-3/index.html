About Water Sort Puzzle 3

Water Sort Puzzle 3 pours fresh levels into the classic liquid sorting formula. Each glass holds layered colors, and you can only pour water onto a matching top color or into an empty glass. Separate every hue into its own glass to clear the board.

Categories: Sorting Games · Puzzle Games

How to Play

  1. Tap a glass to pick up its top layer of water.
  2. Tap a second glass to pour into it.
  3. Water only flows onto the same top color or into empty space.
  4. Finish when every glass contains a single color.
  5. Use the undo button when a pour turns out to be a mistake.

Controls

  • Mouse / Tap — select and pour water

Tips & Tricks

  • Keep one glass completely empty as breathing room for late-game reshuffles.
  • Isolate the color sitting at the bottom of the tallest glass first.
  • If two colors block each other everywhere, undo a few moves rather than restarting.
