About Sport Sort Puzzle

Sport Sort Puzzle is a sports-ball sorting game with football, basketball, tennis, golf, baseball, volleyball, and more. Move balls between bottles until every bottle contains one matching type.

Categories: Sports Games · Puzzle Games · Sorting Games

How to Play

  1. Tap a bottle to pick up the top ball.
  2. Tap another bottle to place it on a matching ball or empty space.
  3. Sort each bottle so it contains one ball type.
  4. Use hints and undo when the board gets crowded.

Controls

  • Click / tap — select and move balls

Tips & Tricks

  • Keep at least one empty bottle available as a buffer.
  • Move rare ball types together early.
  • Do not bury a nearly complete bottle under the wrong ball.
  • Undo is most useful before a chain of forced moves.
